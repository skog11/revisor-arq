# PROMPT COMFYUI: LAMINA INICIAL

## Objetivo

Crear el estado inicial de la narrativa: la misma ciudad canonica representada como un plano territorial incompleto sobre papel. Este activo debe poder transicionar hacia `FONDO LANDING PAGE 5504x3072.png` sin salto perceptible de camara o geografia.

## Prompt positivo

Use the supplied REVISOR ARQ finished paper city as a strict spatial, camera and composition reference.

Create the opening state of the same physical model before its volumes are assembled. Preserve the exact 16:9 framing, aerial oblique perspective and underlying location of every major element in the reference: vacant urban block, central public square, timber lattice building, institutional clock-tower building, curved cultural building, railway, station platforms, waterways, bridges, neighborhoods, forests and layered terrain.

A continuous sheet of warm off-white architectural cotton paper fills the entire frame and extends beyond every edge. Do not show a table, paper perimeter, hands or tools.

Draw a precise territorial master plan using fine graphite, warm-gray technical ink and restrained traces of turquoise and muted green. Show:

- topographic contour lines corresponding to the finished cardboard layers;
- exact canal, wetland and river geometry;
- bridge positions;
- railway alignments, switches and platforms;
- streets and complete urban blocks;
- residential footprints;
- central square paving guides;
- exact footprint of the timber lattice civic building;
- exact footprint of the institutional and cultural buildings;
- the complete vacant block defined by its four surrounding streets;
- forest boundaries using restrained drafting symbols.

The center around the vacant block, plaza and civic buildings is the most resolved area. Outer districts contain lighter construction lines, erased guides and incomplete strokes, but no area is totally empty.

Every line must correspond to a visible element in the supplied final image. Preserve believable graphite texture, varied line weight, paper grain and small drafting imperfections. Warm diffuse studio light, no visible horizon, no text.

## Prompt negativo

different city plan, changed camera, changed geography, relocated vacant block, redesigned railway, changed waterways, missing civic footprints, perspective mismatch, white digital canvas, CAD screenshot, GIS interface, blueprint blue, text, labels, parcel numbers, icons, pins, arrows, hands, pencils, rulers, desk, paper edges, table, raised buildings, finished model, neon, glowing lines, futuristic interface

## Criterio de aceptacion

Antes de aprobar el activo, superponerlo con la imagen final al 50 por ciento de opacidad. Los siguientes puntos deben coincidir:

1. esquinas del predio;
2. bordes de la plaza;
3. huella del edificio de celosias;
4. torre institucional;
5. vias ferreas principales;
6. puentes y cursos de agua.

Si la coincidencia no es suficiente, no utilizar una generacion libre. Crear la lamina mediante line art, ControlNet o trazado SVG derivado de la imagen canonica.
